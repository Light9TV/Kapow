Counterpicks

All four Elite (~15.5M GSP). Terry wins ties. If Kazuya, Ryu or Ken tie for best, all of them are shown. Dittos go last.

Tap a character to add or remove them from your pool.

Terry and Ryu numbers come from Game8's Terry and Ryu matchup charts (-1 / even / +1). Numbers with ~ are estimates: Game8 has no readable chart for Ken or Kazuya, so those are based on how each character plays (Kazuya: crouch dash under projectiles, Gates of Hell and Devil Blaster on weak recoveries, slow jumpsquat vs fast air characters; Ken: rushdown, weaker vs zoners and disjoints). Picks are opinions; adjust to your own results.
